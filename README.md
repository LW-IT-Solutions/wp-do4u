# do4u.eu for WordPress

do4u.eu tools in wp-admin for posts, pages, media and comments, and with WooCommerce texts for many products at once. Started only by you.

- Writing and checking tools in the block editor, the classic editor, the media library, the comment list and under Tools > Website checks, filled in with the content from the WordPress database (also on staging or behind a login)
- Newsletter from selected posts and pages; press releases, checklists and new texts from your facts, saved as new drafts
- Shop tools with WooCommerce: product texts, SEO titles, meta descriptions and image alt texts for many products in one batch, with a free estimate, a review of every field and undo
- Nothing is published and nothing is replaced silently: results stay suggestions until you take them over
- With Yoast SEO or Rank Math active, the SEO tool fills their title and meta description

Needs a do4u.eu account. Requires WordPress 6.4 and PHP 8.1; the shop tools need WooCommerce. Details in [readme.txt](readme.txt).
